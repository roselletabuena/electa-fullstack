## 🏛️ 1. Brand Origin & Naming Rationale

### Why "Electa"?

1. **Latin & Classical Roots**: Derived from Latin _ēlectus_ (to choose, to elect, chosen with distinction). It elevates ordinary "voting" into a prestigious coronation and selection process.
2. **Luxury & Prestige Fit**: Unlike clunky functional compound names ("Electa", "PageantVote"), **Electa** sounds sleek, modern, authoritative, and regal.
3. **Phonetic Simplicity**: 3 syllables (`Eh-lek-tah`), internationally pronounceable, memorable, and visually balanced in typography.
4. **Market Scalability**: While perfect for Philippine beauty pageants and coronation nights, **Electa** seamlessly scales to global awards, student elections, entertainment polls, and corporate talent showcases.

## 🎨 2. Visual & Design Identity

### The "Luminous Opal" Design System

- **Core Aesthetic**: Anti-UI Slop luxury with Aurora gradients, glassmorphism, subtle micro-animations, and 100% ad-free layouts.
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **Headings**: `Outfit` / `Sora` (Bold, confident, geometric modernism)
  - **Body Text**: `Sora` (Clean, legible, high legibility across mobile displays)
  - **Counters & Tallies**: `JetBrains Mono` (Precise, cryptographic data alignment)
- **Palette Tokens**:
  - **Primary**: Deep Regal Violet (`#6366F1` / `#4F46E5`)
  - **Accent**: Imperial Gold (`#F59E0B` / `#D97706`) for winners and top podium ranks
  - **Background**: Luminous Opal Light (`#F8FAFC`) with High-Contrast Obsidian Dark Mode (`#0F172A`)

# Quickstart: Viral Social Sharing Story Generator

## Walkthrough Scenarios

1. Navigate to `/events/miss-universe-philippines-2026`.
2. Click **Vote** on Candidate #01 (Roselle Tabuena).
3. Upon vote confirmation, the **"I Voted!" Social Story Modal** opens automatically.
4. Verify the 9:16 story preview shows:
   - Candidate photo with high-contrast gradient
   - "I Voted for #01 Roselle Tabuena" badge
   - Event title & category
   - Scannable QR code
5. Click **Download Story Image** to verify a 1080x1920 PNG file is downloaded.
6. Click **Copy Direct Link** to verify the clipboard copy and toast confirmation.
